                                                                                                                                                 Received: April 5, 2024                    Abstract. As artificial intelligence (AI) tools have become pervasive in business applica­
                                                                                                                                                 Revised: November 1, 2024;                 tions, so too have interactions between AI and humans in business processes and decision-
                                                                                                                                                 February 17, 2025                          making. A growing area of research has focused on human decision and task delegation to
                                                                                                                                                 Accepted: February 28, 2025                AI assistants. Simultaneously, extensive research on algorithm aversion—humans’ resis­
                                                                                                                                                 Published Online in Articles in Advance:   tance to algorithm-based decision tools—has demonstrated potential barriers and issues
                                                                                                                                                 May 23, 2025
                                                                                                                                                                                            with AI applications in business. In this paper, we test a simple strategy for mitigating
                                                                                                                                                                                            algorithm aversion in the context of AI task delegation. We show that simply changing the
                                                                                                                                                 https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2024.05585    framing of decision tasks can allay algorithm aversion. Through multiple studies, we
                                                                                                                                                                                            found that participants exhibited a strong preference for human assistance over AI assis­
                                                                                                                                                 Copyright: © 2025 INFORMS
                                                                                                                                                                                            tance when they were rewarded for task performance (i.e., money was gained for good
                                                                                                                                                                                            performance), even when the AI had been shown to outperform the human assistant on
                                                                                                                                                                                            the task. Alternatively, when we reframed the task such that the participant experienced
                                                                                                                                                                                            losses for poor performance (i.e., money was taken from their endowment for poor perfor­
                                                                                                                                                                                            mance), the bias for preferring human assistance was removed. Under loss framing, partici­
                                                                                                                                                                                            pants delegated the decision task to human and AI assistants at similar rates. We demonstrate
                                                                                                                                                                                            this finding across tasks at differing levels of complexity and at different incentive sizes. We
                                                                                                                                                                                            also provide evidence that loss framing increases situational awareness, which drives the
                                                                                                                                                                                            observed effects. Our results offer useful insights on reducing algorithm aversion that
                                                                                                                                                                                            extend the literature and provide actionable suggestions for practitioners and managers.
